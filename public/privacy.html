Privacy

Jarvis — last updated 21 September 2026

Jarvis is a private, single-user voice assistant that runs in one car. It is not a product, it has no other users, and it collects nothing for analytics, advertising or resale.

What Google data it uses

With your permission it requests four OAuth scopes, and no others:

  • gmail.modify — to read your mail when you ask what you have, and to reply, send, save drafts, move messages to the trash, archive them and change their labels when you ask for those.
  • calendar.events — to read events in your calendar and to create new ones when you ask.
  • contacts.readonly — to look up which email address a name refers to, so that "email Sam" can work. Read-only, and used for nothing else.
  • userinfo.email — to confirm which account is linked.

What it deliberately cannot do

These scopes are not requested, and each absence removes a specific capability:

  • mail.google.com — so nothing can be deleted permanently. The worst case is the trash, where Gmail keeps a message for 30 days and the app can restore it.
  • gmail.settings.* — so it cannot create filters or auto-forwarding rules. This is the permission that would turn one bad instruction into ongoing exfiltration.
  • the full calendar scope — so it can add and read events but cannot delete a calendar.
  • any contacts write scope, and any Drive, Docs or Sheets scope at all.

Where your data goes

Mail, calendar events and contact lookups are fetched only at the moment you ask a question, and are not stored, logged or retained by this application.

They are, however, sent to OpenAI's API as part of the request that produces a spoken answer — that is how the summary you hear is written. Message bodies, subjects, senders, event titles and locations, and any contact name and address resolved for a message are therefore processed by OpenAI under their own terms. If that is not acceptable to you, do not link a Google account.

What is stored, and where

Only the Google refresh token, held in Cloudflare Workers KV so the link survives between drives. It is never sent to the browser or the car. No mail, no events, no contacts, no addresses and no attachments are written to storage.

Removing access

Unlink from within the app, or revoke it directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking takes effect immediately and deletes nothing else, because there is nothing else to delete.

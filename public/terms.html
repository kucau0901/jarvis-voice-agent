What this is

A personal, private voice assistant operated by its owner for their own use. It is not offered as a service, has no other users, and there is nothing to sign up for.

No warranty

Provided as is, with no warranty of any kind. It speaks to a car, a house, a mailbox and a diary, and it can be wrong about all four — it can mishear a name, summarise an email badly, or put a meeting on the wrong day. Check anything that matters before acting on it, and never let it distract you from driving.

It acts on spoken instructions, which means it can send a message or move one to the trash because it misheard you. Trashed mail is recoverable from Gmail for 30 days; a sent message is not recoverable at all.

Acceptable use

Access is limited to the owner and to devices the owner has issued a credential to. Any other use is unauthorised.

Availability

There is no uptime commitment. It may be changed, broken or switched off at any time without notice.

Contact
